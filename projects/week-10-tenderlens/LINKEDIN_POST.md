🚀 Week 10 of 10: Building TenderLens — AI Public Procurement & RFP Intelligence Engine

Public procurement tenders in India (CPWD, GeM, Indian Railways, NHAI, State PWDs) are notorious: 80 to 250+ page RFPs filled with dense legal boilerplate.

Small MSME contractors spend 3 to 5 full working days reading them, only to discover on page 142 that they fall short of the 3-year average turnover clause or lack the exact bank solvency certificate. Missing just one notarized affidavit or EMD format rule leads to immediate disqualification.

For the grand finale of my 10-Week AI Builder Challenge, I built **TenderLens**.

💡 What TenderLens does:
1️⃣ Deterministic RFP Clause Extraction: Parses Tender Ref, Issuing Authority, Estimated Cost, EMD amounts, and submission deadlines.
2️⃣ Strict Page Citation Grounding: Every single extracted rule is anchored with exact page number and verbatim text quote — zero hallucination, 100% auditable.
3️⃣ Financial & Technical Qualification Engine: Evaluates turnover rules, solvency requirements, and the standard 80% / 50% / 40% experience gate formulas.
4️⃣ Contractor Fit Screener: Bidders enter their annual turnover, largest completed contract, and MSME status to receive an instant ELIGIBLE / DISQUALIFIED / AT-RISK verdict in under 2 seconds.
5️⃣ Mandatory Document Readiness Radar: Cross-checks bidder attachments against required statutory annexures (GST, PAN, EPF, Notarized Affidavits, CA UDIN balance sheets).
