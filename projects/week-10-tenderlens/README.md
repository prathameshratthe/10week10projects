## 📌 Problem Statement
Public tender documents (RFP/RFQ) are notoriously massive (50–300+ pages), full of dense legal jargon, hidden pre-qualification hurdles, mandatory annexures, earnest money deposit (EMD) rules, and strict penalty clauses. Small vendors frequently miss disqualifying criteria.

## 💡 Planned Build
- **Input:** Tender RFP/tender PDF document.
- **AI/Analysis Pipeline:** Long-document parsing with page-level spatial chunking, targeted structured extraction for deadlines, EMD, eligibility thresholds, and mandatory document checklists with strict page citations.
- **Output:** Structured Tender Brief, interactive compliance checklist with verifiable page references, flagged ambiguities/disqualifiers.
- **Evaluation Plan:** Test five public tender documents against ground-truth manually verified facts; measure citation accuracy and zero fabricated deadlines.
