## 📌 Problem Statement
Citizens and grassroots workers struggle to identify which Indian government welfare schemes apply to their specific livelihood, caste/community, landholding, or income status, often facing misinformation or hallucinated eligibility requirements.

## 💡 Planned Build
- **Input:** Multilingual conversational questionnaire (Hindi/Marathi/English) capturing citizen demographic profile.
- **AI/Analysis Pipeline:** RAG with hybrid dense/sparse retrieval against verified, open-licensed government datasets (e.g. data.gov.in GODL terms, official scheme guidelines). Structured output with explicit citation for every substantive claim and strict abstention when unverified.
- **Output:** Tailored eligibility checklist, required documents list, official source URLs, and clear disclaimer that benefits are subject to official authority decision.
- **Evaluation Plan:** 30 answerable and 20 unanswerable questions measuring answer accuracy, citation correctness, and abstention reliability.
