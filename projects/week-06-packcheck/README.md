## 📌 Problem Statement
Procurement and compliance teams spend hours manually flipping through vendor onboarding packets (GST certificates, PAN cards, MSME Udyam, cancelled cheques, ISO certificates) checking completeness, expiry dates, and name mismatches.

## 💡 Planned Build
- **Input:** User-defined compliance checklist + uploaded multi-document PDF pack.
- **AI/Analysis Pipeline:** Multi-document classification, date extraction, checklist matching with explicit page evidence citations.
- **Output:** Pack audit report (Present, Missing, Expired, Ambiguous) with bounding references. Flag ambiguous documents for human review.
- **Evaluation Plan:** Test 15 labeled sample onboarding packs.
