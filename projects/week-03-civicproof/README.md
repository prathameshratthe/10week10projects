## 📌 Problem Statement
Citizens struggle to create clear, actionable municipal grievance reports about potholes, uncollected garbage, broken streetlights, and water leaks. Many reports lack critical details or get duplicated hundreds of times.

## 💡 Planned Build
- **Input:** Uploaded photo + optional location/description.
- **AI/Analysis Pipeline:** Vision understanding for problem categorization, severity estimation, automated captioning, and image vector similarity to detect duplicate grievance filings.
- **Output:** Structured municipal grievance report, verification score, duplicate alert.
- **Evaluation Plan:** Test 20 reports and measure category accuracy and duplicate retrieval.
