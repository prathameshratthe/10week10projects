## 📌 Problem Statement
Small repair shops (plumbers, electricians, auto mechanics, AC technicians) receive frantic audio messages on WhatsApp in Hindi, Marathi, and English. Valuable details get lost, leading to wrong parts, delayed dispatch, and unbilled jobs.

## 💡 Planned Build
- **Input:** Audio recording or voice note in Hindi, Marathi, or English.
- **AI/Analysis Pipeline:** Multilingual speech transcription (Whisper / Gemini Audio), schema-constrained extraction for problem, location, urgency, and parts.
- **Output:** Reviewable work order, editable customer details, printable job card PDF.
- **Evaluation Plan:** Test 20 recordings; measure transcription WER and important-field accuracy.
